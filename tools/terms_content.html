01Who these terms cover

These terms apply to all work Fort Wayne Specialty Welding & Co., LLC (“we,” “us”) does for you, the customer, in our shop or at your site. If a written quote or agreement signed by both of us says something different, that document controls for that job.

02Quotes and pricing

  • A written quote is good for 30 days unless it says otherwise.
  • A price based on photos, drawings or a phone call is an estimate. Once the part is in hand, if the job turns out to be different (hidden cracks, a different material, more damage than expected), we’ll contact you with a revised price before going further. We don’t do extra work without your OK.
  • Rush, after-hours and weekend work may cost more. You’ll know the price before we start.
  • Turnaround dates are our best estimate when the quote is written. We’ll tell you if something changes them. A missed date doesn’t make the work free or make us responsible for what it costs you (see section 11).
  • If we need your OK to continue and can’t reach you, the job waits until we hear from you. The part is held under section 8 in the meantime.

03Your part and its details

  • Tell us what the part is made of (alloy, hardness, heat treatment, plating or coatings) if you know. We plan the repair around what you tell us. If that information is wrong or missing, the result can be affected, and extra work to correct it is billed to you.
  • Parts should arrive with the repair area clean and free of heavy oil, grease, resin and coatings. We may charge for cleaning.
  • Tell us before we start if a part has been near anything hazardous: galvanized or cadmium plating, lead, chemical or food-process residue, asbestos, radioactive or biological contamination. We can decline any part we think is unsafe to work on.
  • By sending us a part or a drawing, you confirm you have the right to have that work done and that it doesn’t infringe anyone else’s rights. If someone claims otherwise, that’s between you and them, and you’ll cover any cost it causes us.
  • Whoever drops off, picks up, or gives instructions about a part on your behalf is treated as authorized to act for you.
  • A weld repairs the area we work on. It can’t fix cracks, fatigue, porosity, wear or earlier repairs elsewhere in the part, and it can’t extend the life of a part that is already worn out.

04Heat, hardness and finishing

  • Welding puts heat into a part. Hardness, color and dimensions near the weld can change, and some distortion is possible even with careful technique.
  • Unless your quote includes them, post-weld heat treatment, stress relieving, final machining, grinding, polishing, texturing, EDM, plating and coating are your responsibility.
  • Weld build-up is left oversize for you to machine to final size, unless the quote says otherwise.
  • We can’t guarantee an exact match of color, texture or polish to the surrounding surface.

05Best-effort repairs

  • Some repairs carry more risk however carefully they’re done: cast iron, castings of unknown material, and parts that are heavily cracked, corroded or fatigued. We’ll tell you before we start when a job falls in this group.
  • These repairs are done on a best-effort basis. They can crack during or after welding, they aren’t covered by the warranty, and charges for the work still apply.

06On-site work

  • When we work at your location, you provide safe access, a clear work area, and any permits your site requires, including hot-work permits and a fire watch if your rules call for one.
  • Before we weld on machinery, you make sure it’s shut down, de-energized and locked out as your safety rules require, and that electronics, bearings and seals near the repair are protected or removed. Damage from current running through unprotected components is on you.
  • We can stop work if conditions aren’t safe. Travel time and mileage are charged as quoted. A trip we make and can’t complete because the site wasn’t ready is still billed.

07Payment

  • Individuals and homeowners: payment is due when you pick up your part, or when on-site work is finished.
  • Approved business accounts: invoices are due within 30 days of the invoice date. Until a business account is approved, payment is due at pickup.
  • We keep your part until the bill for the work on it is paid.
  • Past-due balances are charged interest at 1.5% per month (18% per year), or the highest rate Indiana law allows if that is lower, and you’re responsible for reasonable costs of collecting them, including collection agency and attorney fees.
  • If something on an invoice looks wrong, tell us within 10 days of the invoice date. After that, the invoice stands as sent.
  • A returned or reversed payment is charged back to you with any bank fees we’re charged.

08Pickup and unclaimed parts

  • We’ll let you know when your part is ready.
  • Finished parts are held free for 60 days after that notice. After 60 days, a storage fee applies.
  • If a part is still unclaimed 90 days after the ready notice, and we’ve sent a written notice (email or mail to the contact details you gave us) at least 30 days before acting, we may sell, scrap or otherwise dispose of it as Indiana law allows, apply anything received to what you owe, and bill you for any shortfall. The same applies to parts left after a cancelled job.

09Shipping and care of your part

  • Pack parts securely when you send them. We aren’t responsible for damage that happens on the way to us.
  • Return shipping is at your cost, by the carrier and insurance you choose, or a standard carrier if you don’t say.
  • Responsibility for the part passes to you when it’s picked up or handed to the carrier.
  • We take reasonable care of every part in our shop. Keep high-value molds, dies and tooling covered by your own insurance while they’re here; our responsibility for loss or damage is limited as described in section 11.

10Workmanship warranty

  • We stand behind our welds. If a weld we made fails because of our workmanship within 15 days after you pick up the part (or after we finish on-site work), tell us in writing within those 15 days (email is fine) and return the part. We’ll inspect it and, at our choice, redo the repair at no charge or refund what you paid for that repair.
  • The warranty doesn’t cover problems that were already in the part (section 3); best-effort repairs (section 5); misuse, accidents, overloading or normal wear; heat treatment, machining or finishing done by others; parts changed or repaired by anyone else after they leave us; or results affected by wrong or missing information about the part.
  • The warranty is for you, the original customer, and doesn’t transfer with the part. For on-site work, we inspect at your site instead of you returning the part.
  • This is the only warranty we give. We make no other warranties, spoken or implied, including implied warranties of merchantability or fitness for a particular purpose. Whether a repaired part performs in your machine, process or product is your call and your responsibility.

11Limits on our responsibility

  • Our total responsibility for any claim connected with a job is limited to the amount you paid us for that job.
  • We aren’t responsible for indirect or follow-on losses, such as downtime, lost production, lost profits, missed deadlines or the cost of replacement tooling.
  • We aren’t responsible for delay, loss or damage caused by things outside our reasonable control: fire, storm, flood, power or equipment failure, supplier or carrier delays, illness, or theft despite reasonable care.
  • Any claim connected with a job must be brought within one year of the date the work was finished.
  • Nothing in these terms limits responsibility that Indiana law doesn’t allow us to limit.

12Confidentiality

  • Drawings, prints, models and the technical details you send us are kept confidential, used only for your job, and never published or passed to anyone else, except as the law requires or as needed to do the work (for example, a heat-treat or machining vendor you’ve asked us to use).
  • This doesn’t cover information that is already public or that you’ve shared with us without restriction.
  • Photos of finished work may appear in our gallery or marketing, with part numbers, logos and other identifying marks hidden. If you don’t want your part shown, tell us when you place the job and it won’t be.
  • If you need a signed non-disclosure agreement, send it with your request and we’ll sign before anything is shared.

13Cancelling a job

You can cancel a job at any time before it’s finished. You pay for the work done up to that point and for any material or supplies bought for your job. A rush or after-hours charge already incurred isn’t refunded. The part is returned as-is once that bill is paid.
