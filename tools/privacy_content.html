01Who we are

Fort Wayne Specialty Welding & Co., LLC (“we,” “us”) is a welding shop in Fort Wayne, Indiana. This policy covers information collected through this website and when you contact us by email or phone.

02What we collect

  • When you send a quote request: your name, company, email, phone number, the job details you enter, and any drawings or photos you attach.
  • When you email or call: whatever you choose to share with us.
  • This site doesn’t take payments or ask for payment information.

03What we don’t collect

  • This site doesn’t use cookies, analytics, advertising trackers or tracking pixels.
  • We don’t build profiles of visitors. Unless you contact us, we don’t know who you are.

04How we use it

  • To answer your request, prepare a quote, do the work, arrange drop-off, pickup or on-site work, and send invoices.
  • To keep the business and tax records the law requires.
  • We don’t add you to a mailing list.

05Who else handles it

  • A few services keep this site running, and they handle your information only to provide that service:
    • Cloudflare hosts the site and runs the quote form’s spam check, which looks at things like your IP address and browser to stop bots.
    • An email delivery service passes your quote request to our inbox.
    • Our email provider stores the messages we send and receive.
  • We don’t sell, rent or trade your information. We share it with others only when the law requires it.

06Drawings and part details

Drawings, prints and part details you send us are kept confidential and used only for your job. If you need a signed non-disclosure agreement, send it with your request.

07How long we keep it

We keep your information as long as we need it for your request and your job, and for our business and tax records. Quote requests that don’t turn into a job are deleted once they’re no longer needed.

08Security

The site uses an encrypted connection (HTTPS), so what you send through the form is protected on the way to us. We take reasonable care with the information we keep, but no method of sending or storing information is perfectly secure.

09Your choices

You can ask what information we have about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it, except what we have to keep for business and tax records. Call or email us using the details below.

10Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we’ll update it here and change the date at the top.
