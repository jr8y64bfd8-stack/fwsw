# Homepage (public/index.html)

Scope: the whole one-page site. Visitor mode: Persuade.
Audience: toolroom leads, mold and die makers, machine shop owners with a part down; secondary homeowners/contractors.
Action: send drawings or photos for a quote (email with a prepared checklist), or call when a tool is down.
Proof: real weld photos, AWS D17.1 / D1.6, 25 years, micro-TIG under a stereo microscope. No testimonials yet.
Constraints: plain static HTML on Cloudflare; no invented clients, stats, prices, or quotes; no street address.
Standing preference: the owner chose the category standard (conventional industrial welding site) played straight.

## Direction contract
THESIS: The conventional precision-shop site at full craft: photo-led hero, clear services, gallery, process, contact. Refuses filler stock imagery and generic "welding services" copy; every image is the owner's own weld.
OWN-WORLD: Logo navy field for hero and footer, warm steel-white ground, forest green for the primary action, tan rules and small details. Archivo semi-condensed heavy display, Source Sans 3 body. Square-ish 4px corners, hairline steel rules, real photographs as the only ornament. The 3D two-sided logo medallion turns behind the light sections as the page scrolls.
STORY: Visitor sees a rainbow tool-steel build-up, understands "precision repair for molds and dies", trusts the certifications and photos, and emails drawings or calls.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Header with badge logo, nav, phone. Navy hero: headline left at ~4.5rem, two actions (Send drawings for a quote, primary green; Call), certification line; right half the four-block build-up photo full-bleed to the edge.
FORM: Canon (category standard), chosen by the owner over shop-floor and job-ticket directions; seed key da5265a4.
