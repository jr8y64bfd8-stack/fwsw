Common questions

Can you come to the farm?

Yes, within about 25 miles of Fort Wayne. You handle a clear, safe work area; the rest comes on the truck. Travel is quoted up front.

Can cast iron really be welded?

Often, yes, with the right preheat, filler and cooling. Cast iron is less predictable than steel, so it's done as a best-effort repair and you're told that before it starts.

What do you need from me for a price?

A couple of clear photos of the break and a rough size. Send them through the quote form or text them after a call.

Send the part, get a price.

A few clear photos, the material if you know it, and when you need it back. You hear back with a price and a turnaround.
