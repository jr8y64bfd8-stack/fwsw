Welding for parts that have to go back in the machine.

Most of the work here is repair and build-up on parts that get re-machined to print afterward, so the weld has to be sound, clean and right where it needs to be.

Mold & die repair, tool steel build-up

Worn edges, chipped parting lines, washed-out cavities and damaged inserts, built up in controlled passes so your toolroom can machine them back to size instead of replacing the steel.

Edge work as fine as a single bead along a bore, or full-face pads across a block.

How a job works

No forms or portals. You talk straight to the welder doing the work.

  1. Send drawings or photos

    Email the print or a few clear photos of the damage, plus the material and when you need it back.

  2. Get a quote

    You'll hear back with a price and timing. If it's urgent, call and say so.

  3. Drop off or on-site

    Bring the part to the Fort Wayne shop by arrangement, or the work comes to you.

  4. Ready to machine

    The part goes back with sound, clean weld where you need it, ready for your toolroom.
