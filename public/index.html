Welding for parts that have to go back in the machine.

Most of the work here is repair and build-up on parts that get re-machined to print afterward. The weld has to be sound, the heat has to stay where it belongs, and the part has to be back in your press on time.

Mold & die repair, tool steel build-up

Worn edges, chipped parting lines, washed-out cavities and damaged inserts, built up in controlled passes so your toolroom can machine them back to size instead of replacing the steel.

Edge work as fine as a single bead along a bore, or full-face pads across a block.

How a job works

Four steps, one person. You talk to the welder doing the work, from quote to pickup.

  1. Send drawings or photos

    Use the quote form or email the print and a few clear photos of the damage, plus the material and when you need it back.

  2. Get a price and a date

    You hear back with a price and a turnaround, usually the same day. Tool down? Call and say so; rush work and weekends are on the table.

  3. Drop off or on-site

    Bring the part to the Fort Wayne shop by arrangement, or the work comes to you.

  4. Ready to machine

    The part goes back with sound, clean weld only where you need it, left oversize for your toolroom to bring to print.
